# Accessibility Statement - Notredame Golf Carts
# Commitment to Digital Accessibility and Inclusive Design

## ACCESSIBILITY COMMITMENT
Notredame Golf Carts is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for 
people with disabilities. We continuously improve the user experience for 
everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards to achieve this goal.

## CONFORMANCE STATUS
We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 
Level AA standards published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These 
guidelines help make web content accessible to people with a wide range of 
disabilities and improve usability for all users.

## ACCESSIBILITY FEATURES

### Visual Accessibility
**High Contrast and Color**
- High contrast color schemes for improved readability
- Color combinations that meet WCAG contrast ratio requirements
- Important information conveyed through multiple visual cues, not color alone
- Alternative text descriptions for all golf cart images and product photos
- Scalable text that can be enlarged up to 200% without loss of functionality

**Typography and Layout**
- Clear, readable fonts throughout the website
- Consistent navigation structure across all pages
- Proper heading hierarchy (H1, H2, H3) for screen reader navigation
- Adequate white space and visual separation between content sections
- Responsive design that works on all device sizes and orientations

### Motor and Mobility Accessibility
**Keyboard Navigation**
- Full keyboard navigation support for all interactive elements
- Visible focus indicators for keyboard users
- Logical tab order through all page content
- Skip links to main content areas
- No keyboard traps that prevent navigation

**Mouse and Touch Alternatives**
- Large click targets for buttons and links (minimum 44x44 pixels)
- Adequate spacing between interactive elements
- Support for voice control and assistive technology
- Touch-friendly interface for mobile and tablet users

### Hearing Accessibility
**Audio and Video Content**
- Captions for all video content featuring golf cart demonstrations
- Transcripts available for audio content and video presentations
- Visual indicators for important audio alerts or notifications
- No auto-playing audio that cannot be controlled

### Cognitive Accessibility
**Clear Communication**
- Plain language used throughout the website
- Consistent navigation and page layouts
- Clear headings and section organization
- Simple, straightforward forms with helpful instructions
- Error messages that clearly explain how to fix problems

**User Control**
- Ability to pause, stop, or control moving content
- No content that flashes more than three times per second
- Sufficient time limits with options to extend when needed
- Clear labeling of all form fields and interactive elements
