## PROBLEM-SOLUTION TRAINING PAIRS

Problem: Golf cart loses power quickly
Solution: Check battery connections, test charging system, inspect for electrical drain, replace aging batteries if necessary

Problem: Golf cart makes unusual noise while driving
Solution: Inspect motor mounting, check for loose components, examine brake system, lubricate moving parts as needed

Problem: Golf cart doesn't charge properly
Solution: Test charging port connections, inspect charger operation, check battery condition, verify electrical system integrity

Problem: Golf cart has reduced speed or acceleration
Solution: Check battery voltage, inspect motor brushes, test controller settings, examine tire pressure and condition

Problem: Golf cart steering feels loose or unresponsive
Solution: Inspect steering linkage, check tie rod ends, adjust steering box, verify proper alignment
